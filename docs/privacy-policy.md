# Privacy Policy for My Food This Week

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

My Food This Week ("the App") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Summary

My Food This Week does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter into the App — recipes, ingredients, quantities, and photos — is stored **only on your device**. We have no servers, no analytics, and no third-party data-sharing partners.

## Information We Access

### Camera and Photo Library

The App requests access to your device's camera and photo library so you can attach a photo to a recipe you create. Photos you choose are saved locally in the App's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party.

### Data You Enter

Recipes, ingredients, quantities, and nutrition data you create or edit in the App are stored locally in a database on your device. This data is not sent anywhere and is not accessible to us.

## Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:
- Your name, email, or any account information (the App does not require or support accounts)
- Location data
- Analytics or usage tracking data
- Advertising identifiers
- Any data transmitted to external servers, because the App does not connect to any backend or third-party service

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect or receive any data from the App in the first place.

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting individual recipes/photos within the App, or by uninstalling the App, which removes all associated local data.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it collects no information at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the App adds cloud sync or analytics), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
